BQL export (for MonthlyVavHealth)
---------------------------------
  Export at least: buildingNum (header "Building"), equipName (header
  "Equipment") and inRange (header containing "Range", e.g. "In Range").
  MonthlyVavHealth scores each row from inRange: In Range {ok} = pass,
  Out of Range {ok} = fail, a bad status = skip. Other columns (out,
  band, temperatures) can stay in the export; they are ignored there.
